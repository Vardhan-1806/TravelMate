## Intentionally deferred

These are product extensions, not silently completed features:

- Emergency SOS and live emergency response
- Continuous live location sharing
- Government or third-party identity verification
- Booking and payment integrations
- Premium memberships and partner discounts
- Production-scale community moderation operations
- ML model training and offline recommendation evaluation
- Guaranteed live weather, transport, or road alerts

## Honest product claims

Compatibility is a deterministic, explainable rule-based score. AI output is a suggestion and is not a source of guaranteed live travel facts. Safety tools help users control interactions but do not guarantee personal safety.
